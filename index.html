    How a night runs

    1

    Before the first ball

    Add names, pick courts. Rounds builds the whole night in a second. Late arrival? Add them, the rounds rebuild.

    Between games

    Every player sees their next court and partner on their own phone. The winning pair taps in the score.

    3

    After the last round

    Leaderboard, winner, and a season table for the group. Next week is one tap: same players, new rounds.
